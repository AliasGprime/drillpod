# Third-party software

## Ruffle
DrillPod embeds the [Ruffle](https://ruffle.rs) Flash Player emulator (web build, version 0.7.1),
© Ruffle LLC and Ruffle contributors, dual-licensed under the MIT license and the Apache License 2.0.
The license texts are in `app/assets/www/LICENSE_MIT` and `app/assets/www/LICENSE_APACHE`, and are
shown in the app (About & licences). Ruffle is not stored in this repository: `scripts/fetch-ruffle.sh`
downloads it from npm (`@ruffle-rs/ruffle`).
